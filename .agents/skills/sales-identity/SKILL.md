# Sales Identity

## When to Use

Run this **before** any script, call, DM, or close — it's the pre-script rewire. Reach for it when the operator is undercharging, getting price-haggled, ghosted, feeling needy the moment money enters, or asking "what do I say?" when the real problem is who they're being when they say it. Conviction isn't practiced in a mirror; it's installed. If the energy behind the delivery is still begging, no word-swap will save it.

**Core thesis:** **You don't have a sales problem. You have a self-concept problem.** The objection isn't costing you the sale — your *reaction* to it is, and your reaction is downstream of your identity. Install the new identity first; then the scripts hold.

## Core DNA (the operators behind this)

- **Sheila Bella** (*The Respect Method*) — "Conviction is something you install. It's a system. It's a method… It's not just a word swap — it's a rewire." She undercharged for years carrying an employee mindset; the fix was identity, not tactics.
- **Alex Hormozi** — the **risk-owner identity**: you are paid in proportion to the risk you're willing to take, so the premium operator's self-concept is **"I get paid to own risk others fear."**

## Scripts & Patterns

### The self-concept thesis (verbatim)

> "You don't have a sales problem. You have a **self-concept problem.** The objection isn't costing you the sale — your **reaction** to it is, and your reaction is downstream of your identity. Before any script, we install a new identity." — Sheila Bella

> "You can change every single word in your vocabulary of sales, but it won't matter unless the energy behind the delivery is still begging. Conviction isn't just something you practice in a mirror. **Conviction is something you install. It's a system. It's a method.** … It's not just a word swap — it's a **rewire.** I had an employee mindset." — Sheila Bella (reel DZJbfeJO8Bz)

### Desperation has a smell

> "**Desperation has a smell** and a premium client can sniff it out from a mile away."

> "Their nervous system is reading yours. **Regulated energy holds the price.** Premium clients buy from grounded leaders."

The buyer's nervous system reads yours. Regulate first — grounded, not eager. The flinch you feel becomes the flinch they feel.

### The people-pleaser sales trap

> "The moment money enters, something breaks — you're not giving anymore, you're asking, and asking feels needy."

Diagnostic: if you over-give, over-explain, send the motivational speech and the break-up advice (see `dm-funnel`), you've slid from leading into asking. Sales is **leadership, it's service** — clients want to be led, not pitched-to by someone auditioning for their approval.

### Act like a big deal before you are one

> "**Act like a big deal before you are one** — I called myself a celebrity artist before I had celebrity clients, and it happened."

> "I never said *I*, I said *we*… **If you act like a big deal you'll be treated like a big deal.**" (reel DVRJUcrD95m)

The frame from the 7-step sequence: **"You already have this job. You don't need to try out for it."** Stop auditioning, shrinking, justifying, apologizing for existing.

### Posture, embodied

> "Your price is your posture. And some of you are standing there with your head down, shoulders slumped, apologizing for even existing. **Rise up, stand up, head up.**" — Sheila Bella (reel DY3Y25wJQsT)

### Hormozi — the risk-owner identity

You're compensated in proportion to the perceived risk you take. The premium identity isn't "I hope they say yes" — it's **"I get paid to own risk others fear."** You hold the risk (guarantees, outcome-pricing) because you privately know the odds. That self-concept makes the price feel like a fair trade for risk transfer, not a favor you're begging for.

### The pre-script rewire (run before the call)

1. **Name the current identity** — employee mindset? people-pleaser? auditioner? Which reaction shows up when you state your price?
2. **Regulate the nervous system** — ground before you open your mouth; the buyer reads it.
3. **Install the new identity** — "I am the doctor with the prescription. I get paid to own the risk. I already have this job."
4. **Then** pick up the script (`high-ticket-closing` / `objection-handling`). The words only hold on top of the installed identity.

## Anti-Patterns (what to refuse)

- Reaching for a script when the energy underneath is still begging. Fix the identity first.
- Over-giving / over-explaining the moment money enters — that's the people-pleaser trap, and it reads as needy.
- Auditioning for the client's approval. You already have the job.
- Apologizing for your price or your existence — head down, shoulders slumped, posture leaking.
- Leading with eager, unregulated energy — the buyer's nervous system reads yours and matches the flinch.
- Treating identity work as fluff to skip "to get to the tactics." The tactics fail without it.

## Quality Gates

- [ ] Identified the current self-concept driving the begging reaction (employee / pleaser / auditioner).
- [ ] Regulated energy before the interaction — grounded, not eager.
- [ ] New identity stated explicitly ("doctor with the prescription / I own the risk / I already have this job").
- [ ] No over-giving or over-explaining once money entered the conversation.
- [ ] Posture checked — leading and serving, not auditioning or apologizing.
- [ ] Identity rewire run BEFORE the script, not as an afterthought.
