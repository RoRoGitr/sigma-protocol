# Content-to-Client

## When to Use

Pull this when planning organic content that has to convert, auditing a feed that gets views but no bookings, or building the inbound funnel that does the selling before anyone hits the DMs. If the close rate is low, the fix is usually here, upstream — the consult is a heartbeat check, not a persuasion event.

**Practitioner hypothesis: pre-sale education matters.** The “85%” label is not a measured attribution result for this project. Diagnose offer, audience, content, sales conversation and buying constraints before attributing a low close rate to one cause.

- **Sheila Bella** (*The Respect Method*) — the 85% Pre-Sale Rule, content-as-employee, the posting-poor-vs-rich audit, strategic gifting with required paperwork, and lead-magnet-beats-discount-popup. She built a 7-figure studio on organic content before she could afford employees.
- **Alex Hormozi** — the **Fusion Reactor**: engineer the core customer experience to auto-produce ad creative (rebate-for-video), so the normal function of the business creates the marketing for the business.

## Scripts & Patterns

### The 85% Pre-Sale Rule

Content can establish familiarity and answer buying questions before a conversation. Measure its contribution through appropriate attribution and experiments; the 85% figure is an unverified heuristic. "Stop thinking content is posting. Content is pre-selling — education, conviction, clarity, readiness."

### Content-as-employee (verbatim, reel DVRJUcrD95m) — 1 client → 10 pieces

> "One client, **10 pieces of content** — transformation post, testimonial graphic, talking-head, vlog, FAQ, myth-bust, what-to-expect. **Content was my employee before I could afford employees.**"

The 10-piece atomization off a single client/result:

1. Transformation post (before/after)
2. Testimonial graphic
3. Talking-head explainer
4. Vlog (process/day-of)
5. FAQ
6. Myth-bust
7. What-to-expect
8–10. Researched-hook variants of the strongest of the above

### Posting-Poor vs Posting-Rich audit (verbatim, reel DY-a5YxByth)

| 10k/mo provider (posts to be SEEN) | 100k/mo provider (is SOUGHT) |
|---|---|
| Posts her process — 45 slides on how she does the one thing (nobody cares, engagement dead) | Posts **results** — transformation, reaction, testimonial; shows what happened AND how, with researched hooks |
| Day-in-the-life that's just a documentary | Day-in-the-life that's an **ad disguised as lifestyle** — premium studio, effortless energy, ecstatic clients |
| Advertises her insecurities ("I'm so nervous to share this") | Shares vulnerability **with a purpose**: "here's where it was, here's where I am, here's how I got here" — that's authority |

"One creates noise, the other creates demand. And demand doesn't come from posting more — it comes from posting smarter." Hire a real content creator + strategist (not a college student savvy with social), and **research the hook before you post anything.**

### Strategic gifting with required paperwork (verbatim, reel DVRJUcrD95m)

One free treatment per week for 3 years to **local influencers** — but only with required **paperwork**: a testimonial, a before/after on their feed, a review, and **go live during the treatment.** "It wasn't charity, it was strategy — one free service into 10 paying clients." Never gift without the paperwork attached; the deliverables are the price.

### Lead magnet beats the discount popup

> "A **lead magnet beats a 10%-off popup** — the popup just discounts people who already wanted you; the magnet captures the undecided and builds a list."

The discount popup gives margin away to buyers you already had. The lead magnet captures the undecided, builds the list, and does the pre-selling. Every reel ends with a keyword-comment CTA (comment RESPECT / RX → DM automation) — see `dm-funnel`.

### Hormozi — The Fusion Reactor (customers build your ad machine)

Engineer the core experience to auto-produce ad creative. The loop: **experience → documentation → ads → next customer.** Mechanism: a **rebate** (e.g. $2,500 back, or a free service) for clients who record a testimonial video and grant usage rights.

> "I want the normal function of the business to create the marketing for the business. I want a fusion reactor." — Alex Hormozi

Test whether customer-created material generates qualified demand. Preserve a winning control while evaluating creative and copy; neither perpetual growth nor frozen copy is guaranteed. This is the same machine as strategic-gifting-with-paperwork — the paperwork *is* the rebate's required deliverable.

## Anti-Patterns (what to refuse)

- Treating content as "posting" instead of pre-selling — that's the 85% you're leaving on the table.
- Posting your process / 45 how-to slides nobody engages with (posting-poor).
- Day-in-the-life that's a documentary, not an ad disguised as lifestyle.
- Advertising your insecurities (vulnerability without a purpose / hook).
- Gifting a free service with no required paperwork — that's charity, not strategy.
- Running a discount popup that gives margin to buyers you already had, instead of a lead magnet that captures the undecided.
- Posting more to fix low bookings. Demand comes from posting smarter (researched hooks), not from volume.

## Quality Gates

- [ ] Content addresses a named buying question and has a measurable outcome.
- [ ] Each client/result atomized into ~10 pieces (transformation, testimonial, talking-head, FAQ, myth-bust, what-to-expect, hook variants).
- [ ] Feed audited posting-rich: results over process, lifestyle-as-ad, vulnerability-with-purpose.
- [ ] Hook researched before posting; not relying on volume.
- [ ] Customer consent, usage rights, truthful opinions and required incentive disclosures are documented.
- [ ] Lead magnet in place capturing the undecided, instead of (or alongside) a discount popup.
- [ ] Any proposed customer-content program has approval, a tested hypothesis and clear disclosure/usage terms.
- [ ] Every piece ends with a keyword-comment CTA into the DM funnel.
