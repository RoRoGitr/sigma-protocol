# Game adaptation — Step 1.5: Offer Architecture

Generated from catalog 1.0.0. Canonical source: `steps/step-1.5-offer-architecture`.

Treatment: **conditional** — Runs only when the creator answers that the game is monetized or platform economics matter (paid once, free-to-play, live service, DLC, cosmetics, subscriptions, platform fees, sponsorship). A store submission fee alone is not an offer. When it runs, provenance of sold assets becomes a shipping constraint. 'Not decided yet' keeps this step deferred with a reason and a revisit condition.

## Creator questions

- **monetization-model**: How does this game make money, if at all: none, paid once, optional extras, subscription, live economy, sponsored, other? 'Undecided' is allowed and defers this step with a revisit date.
- **live-service**: Is this a completable work or an ongoing service with seasons or events? If ongoing, what retention loop hypothesis justifies it?
- **sold-assets**: Which assets, characters, music or packs would be sold separately, and what license or provenance record does each need?
- **platform-economics**: Which platform fees, revenue shares, certification costs or age-rating costs apply to the chosen distribution class?

## inputs

- MASTER_PRD game section
- Distribution target class (PC storefront, console, mobile, web, physical) once known
- Company operating map (Atlas 12) for finance and legal owners

## activities

- Run the standard offer architecture only for the chosen model
- Record explicit 'no live ops' or a retention loop hypothesis
- Attach provenance and license requirements to every sold asset family
- Route pricing to the finance owner and terms to the legal owner recorded in the company operating map

## Sources and limits

- [Steam Playtest (Steamworks Documentation)](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/playtest) — verified-source-text. Partner docs read; feature not exercised (no partner account used). PC-storefront specific. Leak risk for unannounced games. Wishlist non-interference is Valve's claim, not measured here.
- [A Theory of Fun for Game Design - author page and 2003 slides](https://www.raphkoster.com/gaming/theoryoffun.shtml) — verified-source-text. Author page and hosted 2003 slides read; the book was not read cover to cover. Weak on fellowship, atmosphere and submission aesthetics. Games-are-not-stories claim is incomplete for presence-driven games.
- [GDC 2012: Sid Meier on interesting decisions](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/gdc-2012-sid-meier-on-how-to-see-games-as-sets-of-interesting-decisions) — verified-source-text. Contemporary write-up read; the GDC video was not frame-reviewed. Meier states rhythm and some puzzle games are a weaker fit. Civilization-heavy examples.
- [ADR 0006: Blender master-asset pipeline (lxsolutions/studio-foundation)](https://github.com/lxsolutions/studio-foundation/blob/main/docs/adr/0006-blender-master-asset-pipeline.md) — source-claim. One company's ADR, not Blender Studio and not this repository's engine choice. Useful as a contrast and a sidecar field list.
- [Sigma Protocol game-development adaptation (architecture decision and integration contract)](../SIGMA-GAME-ADAPTATION.md) — proposed-adaptation. Our proposal, not an external practitioner method. Nothing here proves a game is good. Every phase's evidence must come from an authorized external experiment and a human review, never from the existence of a document.
