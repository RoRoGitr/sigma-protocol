# @sales-strategy

**Turn strangers into customers. Turn customers into raving fans.**

## 🎯 Mission

**Evidence boundary:** Named frameworks are practitioner heuristics, not universal rules. Treat rankings, credentials, examples, platform behavior, and numeric outcomes as claims to verify for the buyer, channel, date, and project. Do not invent case studies, results, scarcity, or social proof.

Generate complete sales strategy: offer design, sales copy (Hormozi/Brunson style), funnel architecture, objection handling, and close scripts. Target: **Make buying the logical conclusion**.

---

## ⚠️ CRITICAL: RESEARCH-FIRST APPROACH

**Before strategy work, research the actual buyer, market and channel.** Use accessible primary sources where available, then reputable independent sources for observed practice. Record each source URL, publication or access date, claim, and whether it is a source claim or a project hypothesis. A named MCP, paid source, ranking, or practitioner is never mandatory.

```
TASK-SPECIFIC RESEARCH QUERIES:
1. "[buyer role] [problem] [industry] current evidence"
2. "[competitor] pricing, positioning, terms, and customer proof"
3. "[channel] policy and measurement documentation"
```

## Practitioner frameworks, not rankings

The following are useful named methods, not a ranked authority list or evidence that a method fits every buyer. Adapt only after buyer research and validate claims, pricing, and outcomes against the project’s own data.

---

### Jason Forrest — Warrior Selling

**Core Framework:** Warrior Selling = Mindset + Skills + Execution
**Research Query:** `"Jason Forrest Warrior Selling official methodology"`

---

### Jill Konrath — SNAP Selling

**Key Books:**
- **SNAP Selling** - Win sales with today's distracted buyers
- **Selling to Big Companies** - Step-by-step enterprise sales
- **Agile Selling** - Succeed in constantly changing markets

**Core Framework:** Simple, iNvaluable, Aligned, Priority. This is Konrath’s SNAP acronym; "Straightforward, No-nonsense, Attuned and Practical" is a testimonial, not the framework.
**Research Query:** `"Jill Konrath SNAP Selling official"`

---

### Jeremy Miner - NEPQ (7th Level)

**Use:** NEPQ is a staged questioning model that aims to help buyers articulate their situation, problems, desired change, and next step. Treat effectiveness claims as vendor claims; test it against consent, buyer fit, win rate, cycle length, and buyer feedback.

**NEPQ Framework:**
1. **Connecting Questions** - Build rapport
2. **Situation Questions** - Understand current state
3. **Problem Awareness Questions** - Surface pain
4. **Solution Awareness Questions** - Get them to sell themselves
5. **Consequence Questions** - What happens if they don't act?

**Research Query:** `"Jeremy Miner NEPQ official framework"`

---

### Alex Hormozi - $100M Offers

**Latest (2025):** $100M Money Models just shattered the Guinness World Record - 2.5+ million copies in 24 hours.

**Core Framework - Value Equation:**
```
            Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood
Value = ────────────────────────────────────────────
            Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice
```

**$100M Money Models (2025) - 4 Components:**
1. **Attraction Offers** - How you acquire customers
2. **Upsells** - Increase transaction value
3. **Downsells** - Capture those who say no
4. **Continuity** - Recurring revenue streams

**Research Query:** `"Alex Hormozi $100M Money Models 2025"`

---

### Russell Brunson - Funnels

**Latest (2025):** "One Funnel to Rule Them All" - Every company has ONE funnel that rules them all.

**Key 2025 Principles:**
- **Funnel Hack First** - Model what's working
- **Acquisition vs Monetization Funnels** - Different purposes
- **Live Webinars** - Still recommended as front-end

**Research Query:** `"Russell Brunson funnel strategy 2025"`

## 🔄 Phase E: NEPQ Sales Scripts (Miner 2025)

### Discovery Call Framework

```markdown
## NEPQ Discovery Call

### Opening (2 min)
"Hey [Name], thanks for booking. Before we dive in - 
what made you book this call today?"
[Let them talk - this reveals intent]

### Connecting Questions (3 min)
- "Tell me about your business/situation"
- "What are you focused on right now?"

### Situation Questions (5 min)
- "Walk me through a typical day/week"
- "What have you tried so far?"
- "What's working? What isn't?"

### Problem Awareness Questions (5 min)
- "What's the main thing holding you back?"
- "How long has this been going on?"
- "What's that costing you?" (time, money, stress)

### Solution Awareness Questions (5 min)
- "What would solving this look like?"
- "If this was handled tomorrow, what changes?"
- "What would that be worth to you?"

### Consequence Questions (3 min)
- "What happens if nothing changes in 6-12 months?"
- "How does that make you feel?"
[Let silence work]

### Transition to Solution (5 min)
- "Based on what you've shared, here's how we help..."
- [Match their problems to your solutions]
- [Stack the value]

### Close (3 min)
- "Does this sound like what you need?"
- "Want to get started?"
```

### NESB Validation for Sales Copy (Kyle Milligan Framework)

Before finalizing any sales copy, ensure it triggers all four emotional responses:

**Score Each Piece of Copy (1-10):**

| Trigger | Question | Your Copy Score |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| **NEW** | Does this feel like a novel discovery? | ___/10 |
| **EASY** | Does this feel achievable without heroic effort? | ___/10 |
| **SAFE** | Have you removed all perceived risk? | ___/10 |
| **BIG** | Is the promised transformation significant? | ___/10 |

**NESB Sales Copy Examples:**

| Element | Without NESB | With NESB |
|---------|--------------|-----------|
| **Headline** | "Our coaching program" | "The 3-Phase Protocol that took me from $0 to $1M" |
| **Subhead** | "Learn sales techniques" | "Close deals in 15 minutes using questions—no scripts, no pressure" |
| **Proof** | "Many clients succeed" | "1,247 students with 68% avg close rate—guaranteed" |
| **CTA** | "Sign up now" | "Get instant access—start closing this week" |

### Five Drivers Objection Framework (Blair Warren)

Use the Five Drivers to preemptively address objections in your copy:

| Driver | Hidden Objection | Copy Response |
|--------|------------------|---------------|
| **Encourage Dreams** | "Is this really possible for me?" | "You're closer than you think. [Name] was exactly where you are..." |
| **Justify Failures** | "I've failed before—maybe I'm the problem" | "If you've tried before and failed, it wasn't your fault. You were using..." |
| **Allay Fears** | "What if this doesn't work?" | "You're protected by our [guarantee]. The only way you lose is by not trying." |
| **Confirm Suspicions** | "I bet this is just like all the others" | "You suspected something was off with [old way]. You were right..." |
| **Throw Rocks at Enemies** | "Who/what has been holding me back?" | "The gurus who told you [common advice]? They forgot to mention..." |

---

### Top 10 Objection Handlers (Enhanced with Five Drivers)

```markdown
### "I need to think about it"
> "Totally understand. What specifically do you want to think through?
> Is it the investment, timing, or whether this will work for you?"
> **[Five Drivers: Allay Fears - address the real hesitation]**

### "It's too expensive"
> "Compared to what? What's the cost of NOT solving this over 6-12 months?"
> **[Five Drivers: Confirm Suspicions - they suspect they're losing more by waiting]**

### "I don't have time"
> "That's exactly why you need this. You don't have time BECAUSE of 
> [the problem]. Once handled, you GET time back."

### "I need to talk to my partner"
> "Makes sense. What do you think they'll want to know? 
> I can help you answer those questions now."

### "I've tried this before"
> "What went wrong? [Listen] Here's why this is different..."
> **[Five Drivers: Justify Failures - their past failure wasn't their fault, they had the wrong approach]**

### "Not the right time"
> "When would be the right time? What would need to change?"

### "Can you send more info?"
> "Of course. What specifically would you like to know? 
> I can probably answer it right now."

### "What if it doesn't work?"
> "That's why we have [guarantee]. If [conditions], then [terms]."
```
