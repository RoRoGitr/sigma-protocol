## Executive summary

High-output content teams run a **brief → research → script → package → distribute** pipeline, not ad-hoc posting. In-house teams that document handoffs ship **2–3× more derivatives per pillar** than teams where one person owns everything.

Sigma Run Track **Content (C1–C8)** owns **production execution**. Marketing **M6** owns the **calendar**; Content owns **scripts, thumbs, atomization, and publish queue**.

---

## Universal content team phases

| Phase | Typical timeline | Key outputs | Run step |
|-------|------------------|-------------|----------|
| Brief intake | Day 0 | Pillar, angle, brand guardrails | **C1** |
| Research pack | Day 0–1 | Hooks, competitors, hashtags | **C2** |
| Long-form script | Day 1–3 | YouTube/VSL script | **C3** |
| Thumbnails + titles | Day 2–4 | Thumb prompts, title variants | **C4** |
| Short-form pack | Day 3–5 | Reels/TikTok/Shorts scripts | **C5** |
| Social copy | Day 3–5 | LinkedIn, X, story posts | **C6** |
| Atomization map | Day 4–6 | Pillar → derivative matrix | **C7** |
| Publish handoff | Day 5–7 | Schedule queue, community handoff | **C8** |

## C1–C8 mapping

### C1 — Brief Intake
**Reads:** M4 messaging, M6 calendar  
**Skills:** `03-brand-voice`  
**Founder today:** Approve brief and pillar for this cycle.

### C2 — Video Research Pack
**Reads:** C1, M2 avatar  
**Skills:** `22-video-research`  
**Founder today:** Approve 3 hook angles.

### C3 — Long-Form Scripts
**Reads:** C1, C2  
**Skills:** `14-video-script`, `18-youtube-script-writer`  
**Founder today:** Approve script before recording.

### C4 — Thumbnails + Titles
**Skills:** `15-thumbnail-prompts`  
**Founder today:** Pick title + thumbnail direction.

### C5 — Short-Form Pack
**Skills:** `19-short-form-scripts`  
**Founder today:** Approve 3 short scripts.

### C6 — Social Copy Pack
**Skills:** `20-linkedin-writer`, `21-twitter-threads`, `23-story-posts`

### C7 — Atomization Map
**Skills:** `13-content-matrix`  
**Output:** One pillar → N platform derivatives.

### C8 — Publish Handoff
**Skills:** `17-community-update`, `content-production-queue`  
**Tools:** the social scheduler MCP for scheduling; media-gen for assets  
**Founder today:** Approve publish queue for the week.
