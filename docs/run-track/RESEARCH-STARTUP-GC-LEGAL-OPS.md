## Executive summary

Legal ops for startups is **formation + templates + escalation paths**, not billable hours on day one. Sequence: **L0 entity formation** → intake/BATNA → MSA/SOW → NDA → IP/trademark → vendor DPA → compliance response → incident narratives → demand letter playbook.

Sigma **L-track** produces **templates and checklists** — not legal advice. Escalate to licensed counsel for filings and litigation.

## L0–L8 mapping

### L0 — Entity Formation (LLC / S-Corp / C-Corp)
**Skills:** `entity-formation-setup`  
**Role:** *Corporate Formation Counsel* (under General Counsel — checklist only, not legal advice)  
**Founder today:** Confirm entity type and formation state; schedule CPA for S-Corp election if applicable.

### L1 — Intake + BATNA
**Skills:** `writing-clearly`  
**Founder today:** Confirm entity name and jurisdiction.

### L2 — MSA/SOW Baseline
**Reads:** S5 proposal template  
**Founder today:** Approve MSA template for sales use.

### L3 — NDA Strategy
**Founder today:** Approve standard NDA form.

### L4 — IP + Trademark
**Skills:** `deep-research`  
**Reads:** M4 messaging, M8 launch

### L5 — Vendor Terms + DPA
**Skills:** `saas-security-patterns`  
**Reads:** M3 stack, O7 vendor registry

### L6 — Compliance Response
**Founder today:** Confirm privacy contact email.

### L7 — Incident Narratives
**Reads:** O5 incident runbook  
**Handoff from Ops on severity 1–2 incidents.

### L8 — Demand Letter Playbook
**Skills:** `deep-research`  
**When:** Contract disputes before litigation.
