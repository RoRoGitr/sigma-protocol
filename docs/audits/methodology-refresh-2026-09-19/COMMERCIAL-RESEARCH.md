# Commercial methodology research — 2026-09-19

Status: **research complete for Steps 1, 1.5, 9, and 10; no protocol files edited by this research pass**.

Scope: primary-source check of the commercial, discovery, positioning, conversion, and feature-shaping frameworks currently named by the protocol. The parent audit is separately handling X/Grok research and the remaining technical/design steps. “Current” means verified against a source available on 2026-09-19. A practitioner’s marketing claim is not treated as causal evidence.

## Executive findings

1. **Hormozi has a material update:** `$100M Money Models` is the third book in the `$100M` series and the Acquisition.com store records its launch/shipping as August 16, 2025. It should be represented as the current Money Models source beside Offers and Leads. I found no official Acquisition.com announcement of a later Hormozi book or replacement framework by 2026-09-19. The existing Sigma skill contains useful Money Models-derived mechanics, but its video quotations and “proven” language need source labels and experiment requirements.
2. **Step 1 should become continuous discovery plus explicit AI-assisted assumption testing.** Teresa Torres’s 2025 guidance says discovery and delivery should be continuous and bite-sized; her January 2026 roadmap describes AI products and smaller on-demand learning units, but does not replace Opportunity Solution Trees, interviewing, or assumption tests.
3. **Step 1.5’s Dunford layer is current and should be retained.** April Dunford’s official site now presents the expanded *Obviously Awesome* edition and *Sales Pitch*, with the five-part positioning model still visible. Treat readiness, audience, scope, champion, alternatives, differentiated capability/value, best-fit customer, and category as decision inputs—not copy templates.
4. **Step 9 needs a stronger message-evidence loop.** Copyhackers, Wynter, and MECLABS support voice-of-customer, message clarity/relevance/value/differentiation, and measured testing. Wynter’s claims about conversion lifts are vendor case studies; they justify a research step, not a guaranteed lift. The current Step 9’s “5-second rule,” “AI discovery scans above fold,” and MECLABS arithmetic should be labeled heuristics/hypotheses unless a cited experiment supports them.
5. **Step 10’s Patton + Shape Up foundation remains useful.** Shape Up’s primary text explicitly describes appetite as a time budget, fixed time/variable scope, boundaries, rabbit holes, and pitches. Patton’s story mapping remains the right journey-to-slice method. Add an AI-agent readiness check: explicit context, acceptance predicates, tool/runtime boundaries, and a vertical slice are execution controls, not a new product framework.
